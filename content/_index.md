Hello! My name is Vou (they/them). Welcome to the online portal into my world. 

I'm a music producer, an audio and software engineer, and a DJ. One of my favourite things to do is sonic storytelling. I love multidisciplinary, weird, and challenging projects that dig deep. I'm on an ongoing journey to decolonize my mind, body, and soul, as well as my art and work. All my music is available to download in high quality for free (or PWYC) on Bandcamp and SoundCloud. I'm open to collaborations, including trades. Don't hesitate to reach out, let's create something meaningful together! :)
